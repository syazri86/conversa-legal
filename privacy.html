Conversa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Conversa is a small, locally run WhatsApp inbox and CRM tool used by its developer for testing. It is not offered to the public.

What data is handled

When connected to the WhatsApp Business Cloud API, Conversa receives the text of messages sent to the connected test phone number, the sender's phone number and the sender's WhatsApp profile name.

How it is used

This data is shown in the Conversa inbox on the developer's own computer so that messages can be read and answered. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

Messages are kept in the memory and local files of the developer's computer. Conversa does not sell or share this data with any third party, other than Meta, which delivers the messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the local machine until it is deleted. See the data deletion instructions.
