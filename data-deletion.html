Conversa Data Deletion Instructions

Conversa stores message data only on the developer's own computer. If you have messaged the Conversa test number and want your data removed:

  1. Send an email to syazri.aris@gmail.com from your address, with the phone number you messaged from.
  2. Your chat history and profile name will be deleted from the local data within 7 days, and you will receive a confirmation reply.

See also the privacy policy.
